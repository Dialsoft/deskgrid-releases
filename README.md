# DeskGrid

**Tidy your Windows desktop into labeled, shaded grids.** DeskGrid is from Dialsoft:

- **Grids:** group your icons into boxes you can name, color and make see-through, then roll up, lock or hide.
- **Folder Portals:** show any folder right on the desktop.
- **Rules:** new downloads and screenshots sort themselves into the right grid.
- **Quick-hide:** double-click the desktop to hide every grid.
- **Peek:** press Ctrl+Alt+Space to bring every grid in front of your windows.
- **Snapshots:** save your layout and restore it with one click.

## Download

**[Download DeskGrid.exe](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dialsoft/deskgrid-releases/main/DeskGrid.exe)** for Windows 10 or 11.

It's a single program file with nothing to install. Put it somewhere permanent, such as your Documents folder, and double-click it. DeskGrid updates itself automatically.
