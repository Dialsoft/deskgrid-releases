# Desk Scout

**Tidy your Windows desktop into labeled, shaded fences.** Desk Scout is from Dialsoft and works much like Stardock Fences:

- **Fences:** group your icons into boxes you can name, color and make see-through, then roll up, lock or hide.
- **Folder Portals:** show any folder right on the desktop.
- **Rules:** new downloads and screenshots sort themselves into the right fence.
- **Quick-hide:** double-click the desktop to hide every fence.
- **Peek:** press Ctrl+Alt+Space to bring every fence in front of your windows.
- **Snapshots:** save your layout and restore it with one click.

## Download

**[Download DeskScout.exe](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dialsoft/deskscout-releases/main/DeskScout.exe)** for Windows 10 or 11.

It's a single program file with nothing to install. Put it somewhere permanent, such as your Documents folder, and double-click it. Desk Scout updates itself automatically.
